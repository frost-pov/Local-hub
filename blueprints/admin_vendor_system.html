Mini contract — key clauses to include

1
Platform commission is 10% of every completed order, deducted automatically before payout. Vendor agrees this is non-negotiable at sign-up.
2
All payments go through the platform's M-Pesa account. Vendor may not accept direct payment from customers for orders placed on the platform. This is the lock-in clause. Critical.
3
Vendor payouts processed weekly every Friday via M-Pesa B2C. Set clear expectations to avoid WhatsApp complaints.
4
Vendor is responsible for product quality and accurate descriptions. Refunds due to misrepresentation come from the vendor's payout. Protects you from absorbing return costs.
5
Platform reserves the right to remove listings or suspend a vendor account for policy violations. Gives you the authority to enforce quality.
6
Vendor grants the platform a non-exclusive license to use their product images and brand name for marketing. Lets you run ads/posts featuring their products without asking every time.
